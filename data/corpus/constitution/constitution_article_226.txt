TITLE: Constitution of India - Article 226: High Court Writ Jurisdiction

DOCUMENT_TYPE: Constitution

DOMAIN: Constitutional Law

JURISDICTION: India

YEAR: 1950

AUTHORITY: Constituent Assembly of India

SOURCE: Constitution of India

SOURCE_TYPE: Primary Legal Text

KEYWORDS: High Court, writs, habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, quo warranto, certiorari

CONTENT:

Article 226 empowers every High Court to issue directions, orders or writs, including writs in the nature of habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, quo warranto and certiorari, for enforcement of fundamental rights and for other purposes.
