TITLE: Mandamus

SOURCE: Curated Legal Knowledge Base

KEYWORDS:

DESCRIPTION:
Mandamus is a judicial remedy that may direct a public authority or other
appropriate body to perform a legal duty when the requirements for such
relief are satisfied.
