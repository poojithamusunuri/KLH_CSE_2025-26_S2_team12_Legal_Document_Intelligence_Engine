# Known limitations

This is an academic, rule-based legal research aid. It is not legal advice.

- **Hand-written vocabulary.** Situations are recognised from a small list of cue words and phrases (12 situation types). Paraphrases outside the lists are not understood; the engine then falls back to ordinary term matching or reports no match.
- **Corpus coverage.** 242 baseline documents plus 7 statutory excerpts. There is no text on domestic violence, robbery, criminal intimidation, FIRs or cyber-crime procedure, so such queries return an honest "no match".
- **Statutes are excerpts.** BNS sections 303, 316(1) and 318; Contract Act sections 10 and 73; IT Act section 43 (selected clauses) and 66C/66D. They are not complete Acts. Verify against India Code before relying on them.
- **Related is not applicable.** A RELATED result is a research lead. It does not show that a law or judgment applies to the user's facts.
- **Small benchmark.** The 20-case relevance benchmark has one direct-tier positive case. Its scores are regression signals, not proof of general accuracy. The situation tests were written by the development team.
- **English only.** Hindi and other languages are not supported.
- **Citation links** show that a judgment mentions an Act. They do not show approval, application or overruling.
